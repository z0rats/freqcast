Why Freqcast

No account. No ads. No catalog lock-in.

Internet radio has quietly survived for decades, but the Android apps around it have grown fragmented — ads, proprietary station catalogs, accounts you never asked for. Freqcast stores everything locally and works with any station that has a public HTTP/HTTPS or HLS stream.

Fully local

Your station list lives in an on-device database. Nothing is synced to a server you don't control.

Open source, GPL-3.0

The full source is on GitHub. Modified or redistributed versions must stay open, too.
