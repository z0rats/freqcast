The core idea

Adding a station shouldn't require a URL inspector

1

Paste the link

Drop in a station's homepage. That's the only input Freqcast needs.

2

Freqcast goes looking

It checks the Radio Browser directory first, then scans the page itself for a playable stream.

3

Verified, then saved

The stream is probed before it's written to your library, so dead links never make it in.

Discover stations

Search a global directory of stations by name, genre, country, language, or what's near you.

Reconnects on its own

If the connection drops, Freqcast waits a moment and tries again — without hammering your battery.

Timeshift

Rewind live radio on compatible streams — missed something? Step back 15, 30 or 60 seconds.

Live track title

Shows the current track title in real time — copy it with one tap.

Sleep timer & alarms

Fade out and stop after a set time, or wake up to any station with multiple daily alarms.

Widget & Android Auto

A home-screen widget for one-tap play, and a browsable station list on the car head unit.

Play it by asking

Play or stop a saved station through Gemini and other AI assistants, without opening the app (Android 16+).

Import & export

Move your library in and out via JSON, OPML, M3U or PLS — switch devices without rebuilding it.

Why Freqcast

Yours, on your device.

Freqcast keeps everything local and plays any station with a public HTTP/HTTPS or HLS stream. No account, no ads, no proprietary catalog.

Fully local

Your station list lives in an on-device database. Nothing is synced to a server you don't control.

Open source, GPL-3.0

The full source is on GitHub. Modified or redistributed versions must stay open, too.

Permissions you can audit

Every permission below maps to a feature you can see. Nothing is requested speculatively.

PermissionWhat it's for
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		Stream playback and connectivity checks.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		Keeps audio playing in the background.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows the media playback notification (Android 13+).
WAKE_LOCKKeeps the network alive while playing with the screen off.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM		Lets wake-up alarms survive a reboot and fire on time.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATIONOnly for Discover's "near me" search — sent for that one search, never stored.
